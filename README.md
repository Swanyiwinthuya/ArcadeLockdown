**Explore the arcade. Follow the clues. Escape before time runs out.**

Arcade Lockdown is a single-player, third-person escape-room game set inside a retro arcade. Players explore connected rooms, inspect objects, collect useful items and solve puzzles to unlock the exit within **15 minutes**.

## Choose your character

Start a new game as **Leo** or **Maya**. Both characters explore the same rooms and find items in the same locations, but their puzzle clues and answers differ.

Character choice changes the locker code, computer password, token sequence, circuit solution and final exit sequence. Some shared tool and fuse tasks remain the same. Each character has a separate best escape time, encouraging players to complete both routes.

## Explore five connected rooms

- **Main arcade:** inspect high scores, posters and arcade machines for clues.
- **Manager's office:** investigate the computer, desk and office notes.
- **Prize room:** collect tokens and work out how to open the prize drawer.
- **Storage room:** search for an item needed to restore power.
- **Power room:** repair the circuit and bring the arcade machines back online.

Clues and items connect the rooms: a discovery in one area can help unlock progress somewhere else.

## Gameplay features

- Animated male and female characters with third-person movement and an adjustable camera.
- Six puzzle stages involving observation, codes, item collection and electrical repairs.
- Three collectible tokens, an inventory and contextual interaction prompts.
- An objective tracker, clue journal and optional hints.
- Detailed arcade cabinets, office furniture, neon lighting and textured room surfaces.
- Animated machine displays and attractions, including basketball and air-hockey displays.
- A countdown, pause menu, Game Over screen and replay system.

Basketball and air hockey are animated environmental attractions, not playable sports minigames.

## The 15-minute challenge

The countdown begins after selecting a character. Exploring, reading clues, using the journal and entering puzzle answers all consume time. Opening the pause menu stops the timer.

Escape before **00:00** to record the selected character's best time. If time runs out, the game ends. Retry begins a fresh escape attempt with reset puzzles and inventory.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W, A, S, D | Move |
| Mouse | Look around |
| Mouse wheel | Adjust camera distance |
| Left Shift | Sprint |
| E | Interact or inspect |
| Tab | Open the clue journal |
| H | Show a hint |
| Esc | Pause during normal play or close an open panel |

Tokens collect automatically when the player is close enough and the token is reachable. Walls and closed doors block collection.

## Art style

The game combines a retro arcade atmosphere, detailed props and textured surfaces with stylized characters. Leo and Maya are late-teen-inspired adaptations of free adult character bases, with customized proportions, clothing and animation setup; they are not photorealistic scans or the paid Teen models.
